Two notes:

- **"Windows 10 Pro" is a known Windows quirk**, not your mistake. The registry
  key `ProductName` still says "Windows 10 Pro" even on Windows 11, so system
  info tools report it wrongly. The real version is 11 Pro 25H2.
- **DDR "799MHz"** is really 1600MHz. Tools report the base clock; DDR is
  double-rate. Shown correctly below.

I could not verify the mouse, keyboard, mousepad or monitor size — those are the
only items carried over from your list. "G503" is not a Logitech model; it is
almost certainly a **G502**. I left your original entries where I had no
evidence to correct them.

## Why this framing

Three things were true and worth saying:

1. **You are a variety shooter streamer, not a single-game channel.** Ten games
   in rotation is the identity, and the description should say so rather than
   listing one title.
2. **You separate the games by mood** — tactical and slow versus fast and loose.
   That is a more useful description to a new viewer than a genre label.
3. **Your clips are a mix, not a highlight reel.** You said laughter and kills
   should be interspersed, not one or the other. The description says exactly
   that, because it tells a viewer what to expect.

The vertical streaming line is genuine too — you run both formats, which most
channels your size do not, and it is a real reason to watch on a phone.
